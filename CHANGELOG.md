# Changelog

Each version of Top & Tail, newest first. The stores update the extension automatically, a few days apart from one another.

## 1.0.0 (not released yet)

The first release, for Chrome, Firefox (desktop and Android) and Edge.

- Skip the last N seconds of a channel's videos, with N set per channel and pre-filled from where the video's end cards appear. Autoplay and playlists carry on as if the video had finished.
- Per channel: start the skip when the end cards pop up, pause at the outro instead of skipping, skip a fixed intro, and set a speed and a volume. Your own speed and volume come back on other channels.
- Add a channel for just a speed, a volume or an intro skip, and its outro plays as usual.
- Add a channel from its own page, not only from one of its videos.
- Never touches ads, Shorts or live streams, and never removes more than half of a video. The popup and the toolbar icon say when a skip is held back.
- With YouTube's Loop on, the video starts again after the intro instead of ending.
- Say what was skipped, with **Play it** to watch it after all, or turn the rule off for one video.
- Backup and restore of every channel to a file.
- Works on YouTube's mobile site, for Firefox for Android.
- In English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese.
- **Report a problem** in the popup: details to paste into a report, which never say which video or channel it is.
